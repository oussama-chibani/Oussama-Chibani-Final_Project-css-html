STEP-BY-STEP SAFETY

Take Action, at Your Own Pace

Simple, practical steps to protect your accounts, save evidence safely, and reach trusted support in Morocco — one step at a time.

Protect yourself online

Online Harassment
What You Can Do

Avoid replying or arguing if it could escalate the abuse.

Save abusive messages, usernames, profile links (URLs), and dates before blocking.

Use the platform’s mute, restrict, block, and report tools.

Ask a trusted person or support line for help if the messages continue or turn into threats.

Specialized Help in Morocco

Platform Reporting Tools: For removing abusive comments and blocking repeat accounts.

Kolona Maak — 8350: Confidential listening, emotional support, and guidance for women and girls.

E-BLAGH: Official Moroccan online reporting portal if harassment becomes severe, repeated, or unlawful.

Cyberstalking
What You Can Do

Turn off live location sharing and review privacy settings on all apps.

Check active login sessions and remove any unknown devices.

Change your key passwords from a safe, trusted device.

Keep a dated log of every unwanted contact, link, or screenshot.

Specialized Help in Morocco

Police (19) or Royal Gendarmerie (177): Immediately if you feel your physical safety is at risk or someone is following you offline.

E-BLAGH: To report persistent online monitoring, tracking, or digital harassme

Kolona Maak — 8350: Guidance on safety planning and local support services.

Threats & Blackmail
What You Can Do

Do not send money, private photos, or personal details—giving in rarely stops the demands.

Save all messages, phone numbers, profile links, and payment requests before blocking.

Avoid confronting the person alone if it could put you in danger.

Reach out to a trusted support service or legal channel as soon as you feel ready.

Specialized Help in Morocco

E-BLAGH: Official portal to report online extortion, threats, and cybercrime in Morocco

Police / Royal Gendarmerie / Public Prosecutor’s Office: For filing a formal legal complaint with your saved evidence.

Kolona Maak — 8350: Confidential emotional and orientation support so you don't face this alone.

Impersonation
What You Can Do

Copy the fake account’s link (URL) and take clear screenshots of the profile and posts.

Report the profile directly to the platform under "Impersonation / Pretending to be me."

Inform close friends or family through a trusted channel so they don't interact with or share info with the fake account.

Check your real accounts’ privacy settings and enable two-factor authentication (2FA).

Specialized Help in Morocco

Platform Impersonation Reporting: The fastest way to verify your identity and get the fake profile taken down.

E-BLAGH: If the fake account is being used for defamation, fraud, threats, or spreading private content.

Image-Based Abuse
What You Can Do

Remember clearly: what happened is never your fault.

Save links (URLs), account names, and dates, but do not forward or redistribute intimate images.

Use StopNCII.org to create a private digital fingerprint on your device that helps block the image from spreading on major platforms without uploading it.

Report the content immediately to the platform where it appears.

Specialized Help in Morocco

StopNCII.org: Free, private digital tool to help prevent non-consensual intimate images from being shared online.

E-BLAGH & Public Prosecutor’s Office: Official legal reporting channels (covered under Moroccan Law 103.13).

Kolona Maak — 8350 & Women’s Support Centers: Confidential psychological support and legal orientation.

Account Hacking
What You Can Do

Start the platform’s official account recovery process immediately.

Secure the email address linked to your account first and change its password.

Log out of all unfamiliar devices and connected third-party apps.

Turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) and never share verification codes with anyone.

Specialized Help in Morocco

Official Platform & Email Recovery Centers: The essential first step to regain control of your account.

E-BLAGH: If the attacker is using your hacked account to blackmail you, impersonate you, or access private files.
