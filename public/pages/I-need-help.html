02 -RECOGNIZE THE SIGNS

Online abuse doesn't always look the same.

It can happen through messages, social media, images, fake accounts, or unauthorized access. Understanding what is happening is the first step toward finding support.

online harassment

Repeated unwanted messages, insults or intimidation.

Cyberstalking

Repeated unwanted monitoring or following online.

Threats & Blackmail

Someone threatens you to make you do something.

Impersonation

Someone pretends to be you using your identity.

Image-Based Abuse

Private images shared or threatened to be shared without consent.

Account Hacking

Someone accesses your account without permission.
